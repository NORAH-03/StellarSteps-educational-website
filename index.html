About Stellar Steps 🌌

Space is full of mystery, beauty, and endless discoveries. At Stellar Steps, we help kids explore the universe in a fun and simple way. Through interactive facts, games, and quizzes, young explorers will learn about planets, stars, and galaxies while building curiosity and imagination.

Next, your adventure takes off! When you press the 'Start Your Journey' button, you'll enter a world of planets. By choosing a planet, you'll unlock a detailed page about it filled with facts and wonders. Then, you’ll put your knowledge to the test in matching games and explore the phases of the Moon. Get ready to discover and explore!
